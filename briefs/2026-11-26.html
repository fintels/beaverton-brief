Action item

Vote Declare 7.42 acres on SW Tonquin Road surplus and negotiate a sale

The county bought the industrial-zoned property at 10620 SW Tonquin Road, Sherwood, for $831,000 in 2016 for the Basalt Creek Parkway project. The county assessor now puts its real market value at $4,060,390. Staff say the county no longer needs it and ask the board to authorize negotiating and signing a purchase and sale agreement; state law lets counties sell industrial land at public or private sale, with or without advertising. The board discussed it in executive session Sept. 22. Budget impact is "to be determined" after negotiation.

Agenda item G.1 (Facilities, Fleet and Parks); tax lot 2S134DD00650

Public hearing

Hearing + vote Vacate County Road 766 inside Beaverton

A 19.8-foot-wide county road established in 1920, in Section 34 (T1N, R1W) within Beaverton city limits. Staff say there's no evidence it was ever built or maintained as a road, it's not in the transportation plan, and adjacent properties keep other legal access. Vacating it would give the City of Beaverton full planning control over proposed developments. Because the petition was signed by more than 60% but not all of the abutting owners, a hearing is required. No known opposition. Beaverton must concur to complete the vacation.

Roads and bridges
  • 170th Avenue bridge over Beaverton Creek: $1.2 million local match from the MSTIP Large Project Match Fund for an $11.7 million ODOT Local Bridge Program grant application (the required match is 10.27%). The replacement would add bike and pedestrian facilities and stormwater management. It's the first phase of a planned three-lane "complete street" between Alexander Street and Merlo Road, with an accessible path next to the Tualatin Hills Nature Park. The Washington County Coordinating Committee recommended it unanimously Nov. 16. The money is held through June 2028; $17.7 million would remain unallocated in the fund. D.12
  • Stringtown Road closure, about 42 days, February to March 2027, to repair Bridge #1280, damaged when a large tree fell on it this year. Signed detour via Gales Creek Road, E Street in Forest Grove and Ritchey Road (about 5 miles). D.7
